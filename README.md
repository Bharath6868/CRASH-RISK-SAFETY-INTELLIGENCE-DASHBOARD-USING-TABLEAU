## 📌 Problem Statement

Road accidents are increasing every year, but **most traffic safety decisions are still reactive**, scattered across static reports and raw tables.

Government authorities and traffic police departments face three major challenges:
- No **single real-time view** of crash risk
- Difficulty identifying **high-risk roads, time windows, and conditions**
- Inability to translate raw crash data into **actionable safety insights**

This project solves that problem by converting **raw national crash data into an intelligence-driven, operational dashboard** designed for **policy makers, traffic police, and safety engineers**.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

To design a **fully interactive, production-ready Tableau dashboard** that:
- Identifies **where, when, and why crashes happen**
- Quantifies **risk concentration and severity**
- Supports **data-backed enforcement, infrastructure, and safety policies**

This is **not a practice dashboard** — it is built to **handle real-world usage**.

---

## 📊 Dataset Overview

- **Rows:** 2,05,000+ crash records  
- **Columns:** 40+ engineered features  
- **Data Type:** Multi-year US traffic crash data  
- **Granularity:** Time, location, road, vehicle, driver, environment  

### Data Challenges Faced
- Inconsistent formats
- Missing values
- Non-standard categories
- No direct KPI-ready fields

> **10+ days spent on cleaning, EDA, and feature engineering** before visualization.

---

## 🧠 Feature Engineering & KPI Logic

Custom calculated fields were created for:
- Peak Crash Hour
- Month-over-Month Change
- Severe Injury Rate
- Highest Risk Road
- Max Speed Observed
- Risk concentration metrics

Each KPI is **aggregated correctly at dashboard level** (no false summing, no misleading averages).

---

## 🧩 Dashboard Structure (One-Screen Landscape Layout)

### 🔹 KPI Row (Top – Executive Summary)
- Total Crash Reports  
- Peak Crash Hour  
- Month-over-Month Change (%)  
- Max Speed Observed  
- Severe Injury Rate  
- Highest Risk Road  

### 🔹 Analytical Views
- **Collision Type Breakdown** – primary crash patterns  
- **Crash Severity Pyramid** – injury severity distribution  
- **Monthly Crash Trend** – seasonality & trend detection  
- **Hourly Crash Trend** – enforcement time windows  
- **Driver Fault Analysis** – accountability insights  

### 🔹 Environmental Risk Factors
- Weather Impact  
- Lighting Condition Risk  
- Day-wise Crash Distribution  

### 🔹 Spatial Intelligence (Core Component)
- **3D High-Risk Location Map**
- Risk intensity represented via size & color
- Auto-zoomed and locked to incident geography
- Designed for command-center usage
